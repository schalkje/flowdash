Red Noses & five new tells.

A second pass on the operations-dashboard validation indicator. The previous round narrowed the field to Pulse Halo and Rotating Siren; both retained for reference at the foot of this document. The five proposals that follow are each drawn from a deliberately different metaphor — propagation, structure, corruption, hazard, seal — so the round produces real choice rather than a family of look-alikes.

  • Locked Pulse Halo · Rotating Siren
  • Asked for 5 conceptually distinct alternatives
  • Constraint indicator sits on input (left) or output (right) edge
  • Audience operations · glance-from-across-the-room
  • Dispositionspike · taste over polish

Five new proposals.

§ I — V following pages
No.01

Pipeline Pulse

linear propagation

The failure travels with the data. Bright red pulses run along the connector line into or out of the node, so the indicator doubles as a directional cue: the pre-failure flows toward the node from upstream, the post-failure flows away downstream. From a distance it reads as a stream of red, not a dot.

motion
three staggered pulses · 1.2s loop
doubles as
directional cue (in vs out)
caveat
needs visible connector stub
No.02

Broken Chain

structural failure

Two interlocking chain links with a jagged break between them — the chain of trust, severed. Iconographic, near-static (a faint tremble keeps it alive), and read instantly from a thumbnail. The absence of red disc breaks the family resemblance on purpose: this one is a symbol, not a glow.

motion
tremble · 0.42s · low intensity
strength
readable at thumbnail scale
caveat
breaks family resemblance with halo set
No.03

Channel Split

data corruption

The disc remains, but cyan and yellow channel ghosts drift off-register from the red core, with rare displacement bars firing through the glyph. The metaphor lands directly: the data itself is corrupted. Best at telling operators "this isn't a system error, this is a contents error".

motion
occasional · 3.4s loop · low frequency
says
"contents wrong" not "system wrong"
caveat
colour-only signal — weak for colour-blind
No.04

Hazard Tape

physical barrier

A diagonal yellow-and-red striped band wraps the failing edge of the node — construction tape across a doorway. The largest indicator in the set, and the only one that reads as barricade rather than warning: not "look here", but "do not pass".

motion
almost none · slow stripe drift (6s)
tone
"do not pass" — strongest of the five
caveat
narrows usable node width by ~30 px
No.05

Cracked Seal

authoritative rejection

A red wax seal stamped on the failing edge, with a jagged crack running through it. Quiet, dignified, document-grade — the crack itself carries the message. Closest in spirit to a paper rejection slip: this isn't a warning, it's a verdict.

motion
none · purely static
tone
verdict, not warning
caveat
quietest signal — easy to miss at a glance

Pulse Halo

radial pulsing halo · the warm, familiar baseline. retained.

Rotating Siren

angular sweeping beam · the loud, emergency-vehicle counterpart. retained.
